Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Calm Kids is designed to support children with wellbeing routines while keeping privacy simple. The app stores progress locally in the browser and does not rely on third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Information we collect

Calm Kids does not collect, transmit or share personal information with a remote server. App activity is stored locally on the device through browser storage.

What may be stored locally

  • Daily check-in progress and stars earned.
  • Zen Points balance and mission completions.
  • Zenzy pet progress and room customisation choices.
  • Settings and simple in-app preferences.
  • Profile choices entered directly into the device browser.

Data deletion

Problem Solver answers are not saved. They are cleared when you leave or complete the activity.

The app includes a reset option in settings. Using that option clears locally stored app data from the browser.

Third-party services

Calm Kids does not use third-party advertising, behavioural analytics or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Calm Kids is built for children and avoids account creation, personal data submission and server-side storage as part of the core experience.

Changes to this policy

If the privacy approach changes, this page should be updated so the current version is easy to review.
